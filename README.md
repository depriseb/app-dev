# My Favorite Series: Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

*"No matter how many people you may lose, you have no choice but to go on living — no matter how devastating, the blows may be."* — Tanjiro Kamado  


I love Demon Slayer because it shows how far someone will go to protect their family.  
It’s not just about swords and demons — it’s about *resilience, kindness, and the strength to stand up again* even after losing everything.  

---

### Main Characters
- *Tanjiro Kamado* — kind-hearted, determined, and always hopeful  
- *Nezuko Kamado* — his sister turned demon, yet still gentle and pure  
- *Zenitsu Agatsuma* — cowardly but powerful when it counts  
- *Inosuke Hashibira* — wild, loud, but fiercely loyal  
- *Kyojuro Rengoku* — a true flame that never fades  

---

### Genre
*Action / Adventure / Dark Fantasy*

---

### Fun Fact
The movie Mugen Train became the *highest-grossing anime film of all time*, even beating classics like Spirited Away.  

---

### Why I Love It
- The animation is absolutely breathtaking — every fight feels like art.  
- Each character has a story that makes you care deeply for them.  
- It reminds me that even in darkness, compassion can be a weapon.
